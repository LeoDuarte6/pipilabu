This sheet was generated from Leo's three 2026-08-09 market-video frames after the procedural v5 and Studio-generated 3D candidates were rejected. It is a tighter reconstruction brief, not evidence that the final Roblox model exists.

## Required silhouette

- One continuous rounded crown. The front silhouette must not produce readable triangular top ears.
- Tiny lateral ear folds are visually swallowed into cheek/crown fur and are almost absent in front view.
- Owner: huge grounded seller mass, heavy neck/chin folds, short muzzle, broad cheeks, low dark eyes, and forepaws that can rest on the counter.
- Customer: belly-low quadruped, long back, almost no leg clearance, tiny splayed paws, and the same facial identity as the owner.
- Owner and customer are separate body architectures. Never obtain one by uniformly scaling the other.

## Roblox translation

- Preserve the large masses in geometry; fur cards or normal maps cannot rescue a wrong silhouette.
- Keep eyes, nose, mouth, paw lines, and cream muzzle/chest as authored readable regions.
- Use the Dog Frog bundle only as a facial/material benchmark: low eyes, broad muzzle, soft fur, swallowed ears. Reject its upright torso and frog hands/feet.
- Base FBX contains the neutral rig and no actions. Export every action in a separate FBX with Copy + Embed, tangents, applied transforms, and the verified PBR map set.

## Rejection triggers

Reject immediately if the result reads as a teddy bear, otter, hamster face, frog, biped mascot, seated plush, pointed fox, skinny-neck dog, or a large head on a generic Roblox torso. Do not promote a candidate merely because it imports successfully.

# Pipi Labu video-turnaround owner v6 - Blender candidate

Status: **CANDIDATE ONLY - NOT APPROVED FOR GAMEPLAY**

This is the first technically importable Blender owner built directly from
`assets/concepts/pipi-labu-video-turnaround-v6.png` and Leo's market-video
frames. It replaces the v5 floating-head/goggle silhouette with a grounded,
massive seller, a continuous round crown, visually swallowed lateral ear roots,
small inset eyes, a short Shiba muzzle, and tiny counter paws.

The second sculpt/export pass is under Roblox's 20,000-triangle per-mesh limit:
17,566 total triangles and 9,510 in the largest mesh. The base FBX contains no
animation take; each action is exported separately with Copy+Embed textures and
tangents. PBR Color, Normal, Roughness, and Metalness maps are included.

The render is materially closer than v5 but is not a claim of final likeness.
Do not wire it into `CharacterPresentation` or publish it until a clean Studio
reimport and gameplay-distance review confirm the face, counter occlusion, fur,
scale, pivots, and animation deformation against the supplied video frames.

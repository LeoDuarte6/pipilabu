# Pipi Labu video-turnaround customer v6 - Blender candidate

Status: **CANDIDATE ONLY - NOT APPROVED FOR GAMEPLAY**

This is the first technically importable Blender customer built directly from
`assets/concepts/pipi-labu-video-turnaround-v6.png` and Leo's market-video
frames. It keeps the customer as a separate body plan: long-backed, belly-low,
four-pawed, tiny-tailed, with a natural projected Shiba muzzle and ear roots
that do not break the round crown.

The second sculpt/export pass is under Roblox's 20,000-triangle per-mesh limit:
16,422 total triangles and 11,302 in the largest mesh. The base FBX contains no
animation take; each action is exported separately with Copy+Embed textures and
tangents. PBR Color, Normal, Roughness, and Metalness maps are included.

The silhouette is substantially closer than v5, but the asset remains a review
candidate. Do not replace the current customers or publish it until a clean
Studio reimport and gameplay-distance review confirm face/fur fidelity, feet on
the ground plane, pivots, scale, and all six action clips.

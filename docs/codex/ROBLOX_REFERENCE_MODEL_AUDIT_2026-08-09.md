# Roblox reference model audit - 2026-08-09

Leo supplied four Marketplace references as anatomy evidence or temporary
placeholders, while explicitly stating that none is the final Pipi Labu look.
All four were loaded into a quarantined, unpublished Studio sandbox, inspected,
and removed afterward. No Marketplace model remains in the cloud place.

## Findings

- [Dog Frog - What the Dog Doing](https://www.roblox.com/bundles/90135622259414/Dog-Frog-What-the-Dog-Doing) is a full BodyParts bundle. Its broad face, fur treatment, and swallowed ears are the strongest facial reference, but its biped torso, frog hands, and upright proportions fail both owner and customer architectures.
- [What the dog doin Pipilabu](https://www.roblox.com/catalog/92943054343364/What-the-dog-doin-Pipilabu) is a Hat accessory. It contains a useful very-low dog/blob silhouette, but the shield and knife are part of the accessory presentation, so it is unsuitable as a clean gameplay body.
- [doge meme funny 67](https://www.roblox.com/catalog/95046427956515/doge-meme-funny-67) is a ShoulderAccessory. Its compact mass is reference-only; it does not provide the target Shiba face or usable character architecture.
- [Cheems](https://www.roblox.com/bundles/227001796848590/Cheems) is a full BodyParts bundle. Its coat volume is useful evidence, but the readable top ears and standard upright avatar anatomy directly contradict the supplied video frames.

## Locked translation

Borrow surface cues, cheek width, coat softness, and compact mass. Do not borrow
upright R15 anatomy, frog extremities, weapons, crown-breaking ears, or accessory
attachment assumptions. The owner remains the huge natural seller behind the
counter; customers remain tiny belly-low quadrupeds. The market-video frames and
`assets/concepts/pipi-labu-video-turnaround-v6.png` outrank every Marketplace
reference.
